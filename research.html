The Borlaug Reform: A Geopolitical Reaction to Global Hunger and the Green Revolution of the 1960s

National History Day 2026 · placed 6th at the national contest

abstract

This paper looks at Norman Borlaug's Green Revolution as a reform shaped by the Cold War. It follows how new high-yield crops spread in the 1960s and where agricultural science and Cold War politics overlapped.
