About

Two passions,
one throughline.

Hi! I'm Anna, from Johnston, Iowa. I'm a boarding student at Phillips Academy in Andover, Massachusetts, where I serve as co-president of the Andover Economics Society, head of fundraising for our UNICEF chapter, and play varsity tennis.

My work spans agricultural policy and economics research — from programming software solutions for agricultural equipment at John Deere to presenting an economic proposal on food insecurity in Afghanistan as a Borlaug Scholar at the World Food Prize Global Youth Institute.

I'm also a classical pianist studying under Victor Cayres at the New England Conservatory Preparatory School on full scholarship — a journey that has taken me from Beijing to Macau to Carnegie Hall.

Whether it's music, research, or organizing, the throughline is the same: using what I love to serve people — raising funds for the arts in Iowa, tutoring middle schoolers in math, and advocating for food security worldwide.
