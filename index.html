Andover Economic Review

read the journal →

I'm the editor in chief of the Andover Economic Review, an economics journal written by and for high school students.

This cycle we received over 90 papers from students in 23 countries, and our team of four editors is reading through them now. The journal is funded by an Abbot Grant, and we're working on an alumni endowment so it can keep going after we graduate. If you're a high school student writing about economics, we'd love to read your work.
